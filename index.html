Why Must I Cry:

Run the moonlit maze, collect glowing tears, dodge dramatic trouble, and sing your way out.

How to Play

Collect every tear.

Move: arrow keys on desktop, touch pad on mobile.

Goal: collect glowing tears. Music notes add bonus points.

Danger: enemies chase you. Three catches ends the run.

Power-ups: soap clears nearby enemies, cry freezes them, Maximo makes you invincible, shield blocks one hit.
